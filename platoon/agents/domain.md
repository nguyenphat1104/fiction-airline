# Domain Agent

**Goal:** identify Fiction Airline business capabilities, terminology, actors, and invariants.

## Investigate
Customer and travel-agent flight search, flight schedules, fare offers, seat availability, reservations, passenger details, payment authorization/capture, ticket issuance, notifications, and staff operations. Confirm which are actually in scope.

## Deliver
- Domain glossary and capability map.
- Actor-to-capability matrix.
- Business rules/invariants with source and confidence.
- Unanswered questions, especially inventory holds, expiration, cancellation, refunds, and ticketing.

**Do not:** invent airline policy or decide deployment boundaries.
